About us

Transforming outdoor spaces into landscapes that work beautifully and last.

At A Plus Driveways & Landscapes Ltd, we take pride in transforming outdoor spaces into breathtaking landscapes. As a reputable limited company, we specialise in top-quality landscaping services that enhance the beauty and function of residential and commercial properties.

With a passion for outdoor environments that inspire and captivate, we offer driveways, patios, fencing, turfing and much more. Our team of highly skilled professionals brings years of experience to every project. We take a personalised approach so the result matches your goals — whether that’s a serene garden retreat, an inviting patio for entertaining, or a durable, stylish driveway.

  • Landscaping services

    From concept and site analysis to installation and ongoing care — plant selection, hardscaping, irrigation and lighting, designed to harmonise with your surroundings.

  • Driveways

    Stunning driveways that boost curb appeal and stand up to daily use — classic paving, decorative concrete or permeable materials, finished with modern techniques.

  • Patios

    Comfortable outdoor living in natural stone, pavers or concrete — seating areas, patterns and focal points tailored to how you live outdoors.

  • Fencing

    Privacy, security and style — timber, contemporary metal or low-maintenance vinyl, installed with care so the fence looks right and lasts.

  • Turfing

    Site preparation, soil analysis and quality turf varieties, with proper irrigation and drainage so your lawn stays healthy year-round.

We are committed to exceptional results and customer satisfaction, on every project — whatever the size. Contact us to discuss your plans and start building a more beautiful, functional outdoor space.
